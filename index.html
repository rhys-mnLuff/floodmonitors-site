04HOW IT WORKS

From the ground to your phone in three steps.

STEP 1
Nodes read the ground

Each solar node reads soil moisture and surface water on one cycle, then scores it 0–100 against that site’s own wilting point and saturation.

STEP 2
Radio to one base station

Readings travel over free 433MHz radio, up to 340m in saturated ground, to a single base station. No WiFi or internet at the field edge.

STEP 3
SMS to your phone

The base station’s one SIM texts you when a field goes saturated or standing water rises. Text STATUS any time for current numbers and a map pin.

Specs at a glance
SENSING
Capacitive soil probe and a water level sensor on every node
OUTPUT
Raw soil moisture plus a 0–100 condition index per node
RADIO
433MHz node-to-base, 340m verified in saturated rice paddies at 1200bps
UPLINK
Standard cellular SMS. One SIM per base station, none per sensor
POWER
Solar with internal batteries that carry each module through several days without direct sun
ALERTS
Automatic texts on saturation and rising standing water. Dry-side alerts are on the roadmap
NOT MEASURED
Nutrients, pH and soil temperature. FMS reads moisture and standing water only
